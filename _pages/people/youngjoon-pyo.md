Youngjoon Pyo is a graduate student interested in how changes in pH reshape
the interactions between RNA and proteins. He contributed to the lab's study
of exogenous RNA surveillance by TRIM25, a proton-sensing protein that helps
cells tell foreign RNA, such as that of viruses, from their own (*Science*, 2025).

He is now asking which other proteins change how they bind RNA as pH shifts,
and what this means for the cell.
