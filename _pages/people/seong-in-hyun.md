Seong-In Hyun is a postdoctoral fellow with a background in virology
and human genetics. Seong-In's doctoral research, with Bernard Moss,
examined how vaccinia virus builds its virions, from the topology of viral
membrane proteins (*Traffic*, 2015) to the role of the I2 protein in virion
morphogenesis (*Journal of Virology*, 2017). Before joining the lab, Seong-In had worked in
clinical genomics for the diagnosis of rare genetic
disorders.
