Youngran Park is a postdoctoral researcher studying exogenous RNA biology,
spanning RNA viruses and RNA therapeutics. During her doctoral research at
Johns Hopkins University, she investigated the role of ARID1A in DNA repair
and how its dysfunction contributes to cancer development and therapeutic
vulnerabilities, particularly in ovarian cancer (*Clinical Cancer Research*,
2019; *Laboratory Investigation*, 2021).

Recognizing the potential of RNA as a platform for personalized medicine,
she transitioned to RNA biology. In this lab, Youngran has explored interactions
between viral RNAs and viral or host proteins (*Molecular Cell*, 2021;
*Nucleic Acids Research*, 2025). She also led a study identifying
the SARS-CoV-2 genome packaging signal (*Nature Communications*, 2026).

Youngran's current research focuses on developing next-generation RNA therapeutics.
By learning from sophisticated strategies shaped by millions of years of viral
evolution, she aims to develop innovative RNA technologies that improve human health.
