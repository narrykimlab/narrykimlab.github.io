Hyojin Park is assistant to the center's director and manages its
facilities, which between them cover most of what keeps the center
running. Visiting speakers find their seminar room booked, their day
planned and the lab tour ready, and leaks, stuck doors and fire alarms find
their repair crew before most of the lab has noticed anything was wrong.

When the center moved into its present building, Hyojin had the doors,
keys and card readers working before the last boxes were unpacked.
